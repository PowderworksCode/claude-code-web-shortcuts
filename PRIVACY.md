# Privacy Policy

Claude Code Web Shortcuts does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

The extension stores your configured buttons and keyboard shortcuts locally using your browser's `chrome.storage.sync` API, so your settings follow your browser profile. This data is accessible only to you through your browser and is never sent anywhere else.

The extension's content script runs only on pages served from `https://claude.ai`. It reads the current page's DOM only to insert the quick-reply buttons and to read and update the text of the composer when you click a button or press its chord. It does not read or transmit any other content from those pages.

The extension makes no network requests of its own.

Source: https://github.com/PowderworksCode/claude-code-web-shortcuts
